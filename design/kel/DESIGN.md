Authority: approved Figma exports in reference/ and the user's 2026-09-25 specification.
Use Instrument Sans headings/UI and Inter body. The user overrides older Figma SF Pro guidance.
Use deep navy #0B1734, blue #2F6BE0, amber #FCAC51, card titles #FFC481.
Keep the original PLPD mesh declaration byte-for-byte. Add blue wash and 168px header fade.

Use blue selection. Status labels are bold colored text, never chips. Use names, never avatar bubbles.
Body and meaningful metadata remain at least 14px. Only section labels may use the specified 12px.
Keep focus rings, keyboard interaction, opaque menus, readable overlays, reduced-motion support, and light-mode functionality.
